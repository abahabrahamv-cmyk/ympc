Score Ranges & What They Mean

0–4 "NO" or "I DON'T KNOW"

Good shape / Keep it up
You demonstrate strong financial intelligence — solid habits, awareness, planning, and control. You're likely building wealth steadily. Continue refining and protecting what you've built (diversification, legacy planning, giving).

5–10 "NO" or "I DON'T KNOW"

On the right track / Needs to improve
You're moving in the right direction but have noticeable gaps (perhaps in budgeting, investing, emergency funds, or long-term planning). Focus on closing 2–3 weak areas — small consistent changes here can move you into the "good shape" zone quickly.

11–12 "NO" or "I DON'T KNOW"

Weak pulse / Needs revival
Serious gaps exist in core financial habits, awareness, or security. Your financial health is fragile — urgent action is needed (start tracking income/expenses, build emergency savings, define clear goals) to avoid stress or crisis.

13+ "NO" or "I DON'T KNOW"

Needs a life.
Major financial blind spots are present across awareness, habits, planning, and protection. This score signals high risk of financial struggle. The good news: awareness is the first step. Start small — track every rupee, learn basic budgeting, build a small emergency fund — and seek mentorship/books like The School of Money.

Score = total number of NO + I DON'T KNOW answers (out of 26)
